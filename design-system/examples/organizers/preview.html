WordCamp Mumbai is planned and run entirely by volunteers from the Mumbai WordPress community. Developers, designers, marketers, educators and business owners who give their evenings and weekends so the community can meet for a day.

Say hello when you see them at the venue. They are the friendly faces making 13 February 2027 happen.
