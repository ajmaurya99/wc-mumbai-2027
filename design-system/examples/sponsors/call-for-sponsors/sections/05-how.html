How it works

Three steps to being on the banner

01

Tell us your tier

Fill in the sponsorship form with your company details and the package you have in mind. Not sure? Say so; we will help you choose.

02

WordCamp Central invoices you

Sponsorship payments go through WordCamp Central’s official process, never through the organisers or personal channels.

03

Send us your logo

We add you to the website, the signage and the announcements, and confirm your passes and sponsor space.
