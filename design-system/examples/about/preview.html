What is a WordCamp?

WordCamps are informal, community-organised conferences about WordPress. They are affordable, welcoming to beginners and run by volunteers from the local community, with support from the global WordPress community programme.

Who organises WordCamp Mumbai?

A team of twelve volunteers from the Mumbai WordPress meetup community, in their evenings and weekends. Meet them here.

How much does it cost to attend?

WordCamp tickets are kept deliberately affordable; sponsors cover most of what it takes to run the day. Ticket details and the on-sale date will be announced in the news.

How can I get involved?

Sponsor, speak, volunteer or simply attend. The call for sponsors is open now; the calls for speakers and volunteers follow soon.
