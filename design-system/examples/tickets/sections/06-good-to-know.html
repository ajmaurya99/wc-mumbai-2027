Good to know

Before you buy

The short answers to the questions we get asked most. Anything else, write to us and we will get back to you.

Speaking, sponsoring or volunteering? The call for speakers and the call for sponsors are open now.

Which tickets are on sale?

Early bird tickets are on sale now, on this page. Early bird is a limited batch; regular tickets follow once it is gone.

How much does a ticket cost?

Early bird tickets are ₹650. WordCamp tickets are kept deliberately affordable; our sponsors cover most of what it takes to run the day.

Do I need an account to buy a ticket?

Yes. Tickets are sold through WordCamp.org, so you log in with your WordPress.org account before choosing a ticket. This is the account for the official WordPress.org website, not your personal WordPress site. Create one here if you do not have one yet.

How can I pay?

We accept all major cards and UPI. Your confirmation and receipt arrive by email as soon as the payment goes through.

Can I transfer my ticket or get a refund?

Each ticket admits one person. Attendee details can be edited from the link in your confirmation email, so a ticket can be passed on to someone else. Refund terms will be published alongside the tickets when sales open.

What do I need on the day?

A digital copy of your confirmation email and a valid photo ID. Collect your badge at the registration desk and wear it throughout the event. By buying a ticket you agree to the Code of Conduct.
