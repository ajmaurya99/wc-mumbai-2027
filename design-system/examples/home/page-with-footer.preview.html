Key facts

Mumbai becomes a meeting point for the WordPress community

Aamchi Mumbai, Aapla WordPress

On 13 February 2027, Mumbai brings together developers, designers, creators, agencies, entrepreneurs, product builders, educators and business owners for one day of WordPress.

Whether you build sites for a living, run a business on WordPress or are just getting started, there is a place for you here.
