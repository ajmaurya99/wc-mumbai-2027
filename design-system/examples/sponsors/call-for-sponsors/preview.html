FAQ

Questions sponsors ask

If yours is not here, the sponsor team is one email away.

Who will we get to meet at WordCamp Mumbai?

WordCamp Mumbai brings together developers, designers, freelancers, agency owners, founders, students, content creators and WordPress users from Mumbai and beyond.

As a sponsor, you get the opportunity to put your brand in front of people who actively build websites, run businesses, create content and work with WordPress every day.

Which sponsorship tier is right for us?

Take a look at the sponsorship tiers and the comparison table to see what each package offers.

Not sure which one works best for your goals? Our sponsor team will be happy to help you choose. Write to us at mumbai@wordcamp.org.

Can we sponsor through products or services instead of money?

Yes. We are happy to consider in-kind sponsorships where a company provides products or services that help us run the event. This could include refreshments, merchandise, the after-party or side events.

We will work with you to agree on the value of the contribution and the sponsorship benefits that come with it.

Does sponsorship give us a speaking opportunity?

No. Sponsorship and speaking are separate. WordCamp sessions are selected through the event’s speaker-selection process.

Sponsors get the visibility and recognition included in their package, along with opportunities to connect with attendees through their sponsor space and other event activities.

Will we receive attendee contact details or a lead list?

No. We do not share attendee contact details or lead lists with sponsors.

Instead, sponsors connect with attendees directly through conversations, networking and their sponsor space. That is how genuine connections with people interested in your brand are built.

What if our team cannot attend the event?

That’s okay. You can still make use of the sponsorship benefits included in your package.

Your branding, website visibility, social media benefits and other agreed deliverables can still be provided. Our sponsor team can also coordinate the required branding materials if your team cannot be there in person.

How does the sponsorship payment work?

Sponsorship payments are handled through the official WordCamp Central payment process. Once your sponsorship is confirmed, you will receive the payment instructions.

The organising team does not accept sponsorship payments through informal or personal channels.

How can we become a sponsor?

Fill in the WordCamp Mumbai sponsorship form with your company details, preferred sponsorship tier and any questions you may have.

Our sponsor team will get in touch to discuss the package, confirm availability and take you through the next steps.

Ready to partner with us?

Fill in the short sponsor application and the team will get back to you within two working days.
