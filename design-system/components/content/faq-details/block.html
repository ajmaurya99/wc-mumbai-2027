How much do tickets cost?

Tickets are ₹1,200 and include lunch, tea and the after party. Micro-sponsor tickets help us cover the gap.

Is there a Contributor Day?

Yes, on the day before the camp. It is free, beginner-friendly and open to anyone with a WordPress.org account.

Can I speak at WordCamp Mumbai?

The call for speakers opens in November. First-time speakers are welcome; we run a mentoring round before selection.
