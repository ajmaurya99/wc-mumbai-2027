The city

A city of many worlds

Fast, ambitious, diverse, creative and endlessly collaborative

Mumbai has always been a city of many worlds. WordCamp Mumbai brings that same spirit to the WordPress community: one day where the ideas, conversations, creativity and connections that make Mumbai and WordPress special come together.

01 Learn

Talks and workshops from the people who build with WordPress every day, whether you are on your first theme or your hundredth client site.

02 Share

Bring what you know. Lightning talks, hallway conversations and Contributor Day are where the community teaches itself.

03 Connect

Freelancers, agencies, product teams, students and first-timers in one room. Meet the people who understand your world.

04 Build

Discover new tools, new collaborators and new opportunities, and leave with something to start on Monday.
