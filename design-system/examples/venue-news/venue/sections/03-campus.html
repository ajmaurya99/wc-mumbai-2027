The campus

A space built for learning and connecting

The campus has modern infrastructure and spacious facilities that suit a WordPress conference well: dedicated spaces for talks and workshops, and areas where attendees can meet, have conversations and network with fellow members of the community.

Whether you build sites for a living, exchange ideas with fellow designers, run an agency looking for collaborators or are attending your first WordCamp, the venue gives you plenty of room to connect.

The session may be 30 minutes. The hallway conversation could stay with you for years.
